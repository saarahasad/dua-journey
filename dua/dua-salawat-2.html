Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammad wa 'ala azwajihi wa dhurriyyatihi kama sallayta 'ala aali Ibrahim. Allahumma barik 'ala Muhammad wa 'ala azwajihi wa dhurriyyatihi kama barakta 'ala aali Ibrahim innaka Hamidun Majid.

O Allah, send prayers upon Muhammad and upon his wives and his offspring as You sent prayers upon the family of Ibrahim. O Allah, bless Muhammad and his wives and his offspring as You blessed the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are Praiseworthy, Glorious.
