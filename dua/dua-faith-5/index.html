Rabbana waj'alna muslimayni laka wa min dhurriyyatina ummatan muslimatan laka wa arina manasikana wa tub 'alayna innaka Antat-Tawwabur-Rahim.

Our Lord, make us submissive to You and from our descendants a nation submissive to You. Show us our rites and accept our repentance. Indeed, You are the Accepting of Repentance, the Most Merciful.
